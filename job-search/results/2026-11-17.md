### Knit Health — MEDIUM (Series A, MD co-founder in a research role)
- **Raised:** $35M Series A, announced Nov 16, 2026. NEA led, with Town Hall Ventures, SignalFire and existing investors (Uncork Capital, Frist Cressey Ventures). Total raised is $46.6M.
- **Product:** Med-BEAM, a "world model" for healthcare. A UC Berkeley spinout, it is trained on EMR data covering more than 130M patients. It predicts a patient's next care needs (diagnoses, orders, referrals) and evaluates interventions. It is being deployed commercially to guide patient referrals and care routing.
- **MD on team:** Yes, but only on the research side. Co-founder and Chief Scientist Maya Petersen, MD, PhD, is a Berkeley epidemiologist and biostatistician. There is no CMO or clinical commercial leader. CEO Jonathan Kolstad is a health economist, and COO Ted Robertson is a co-founder.
- **Suggested contact:** Jonathan Kolstad, CEO (LinkedIn not verified). Alternatively, the NEA partner who led the round, or Town Hall Ventures, which focuses on care delivery.
- **Rationale:** The company is moving from research to commercial deployment in health systems. That typically creates a need for a physician who can own clinical validation, clinician-facing product decisions and health-system sales. This is the overlap of clinical work, product and go-to-market where an EM physician and former health AI founder fits best. Acute-care prediction (24h inpatient action prediction) is also core EM territory.
