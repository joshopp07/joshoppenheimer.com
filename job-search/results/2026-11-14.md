## Outreach Opportunities

### Duet — HIGH (Series A, no MD on leadership found)
- **Raised:** $15M Series A, announced Nov 12, 2026, led by Kairos with Lerer Hippeau. Total raised is $22.5M.
- **Product:** A network of primary care practices owned by nurse practitioners (NPs). Duet provides a vertically integrated platform for them. Its AI finds patients who have fallen out of care and flags critical issues before appointments. It also handles billing, credentialing and payer contracting. Duet plans to launch an NP-led Medicare ACO in 2027.
- **MD on team:** None found. Jonathan Goldberg is founder and CEO, and the company describes itself as led by entrepreneurs and NPs.
- **Suggested contact:** Jonathan Goldberg, founder/CEO (LinkedIn not verified). Alternatively, the partner at Kairos who led the round.
- **Rationale:** The company is building a primary care network and an ACO launching in 2027. That creates a need for physician-level clinical governance, quality and safety oversight of its AI flags, and ACO medical leadership. A generalist EM physician with founder experience fits well, possibly as a fractional CMO. Caveat: the brand is NP-led, so frame the pitch as supporting NPs, not supervising them.
- Sources: https://healthcareittoday.com/2026/11/13/duet-raises-15m-vumedi-receives-104-million-investment-from-oak-hc-ft , https://healthtechrounds.substack.com/p/healthtech-rounds-05-vumedi-raises

### Vumedi — MEDIUM (Series B)
- **Raised:** $104M growth investment from Oak HC/FT, reported Nov 9–13, 2026. Some sources call it a Series B. The figure is unverified: CB Insights shows an ~$80M Series B in May 2025, so this may be an extension.
- **Product:** A peer-to-peer video learning platform for physicians. Its AI makes more than 100K physician-led videos searchable to support clinical decision-making.
- **MD on team:** None found in public sources. Roman Giverts is founder and CEO.
- **Suggested contact:** Roman Giverts, CEO. Alternatively, Oak HC/FT, whose partner Vig Chandramouli was also the contact for Alterra Labs.
- **Rationale:** A large raise for a physician-facing AI product could create clinical content, strategy or partnerships leadership roles. However, it is an education and content product rather than point-of-care care delivery, so the fit is moderate.
